Everything a player needs

From the first brew to game night.

layers

Deck builder

Commander and every other format. Mana curve and color stats, mana base help, draw odds, combo finder, bracket estimate and budget swaps. Import from Archidekt, Moxfield or any text list.

photo_camera

Card scanner

Point your camera at your cards and they're added with the right set, language and finish, straight into a binder, list or deck.

inventory_2

Collection tracker

Folders and lists, what your collection is worth and how that changes over time, price drops, and CSV import and export.

search

Card search

Fast search with full filters, every printing, rulings, and prices from Card Kingdom, TCGplayer and Cardmarket.

playing_cards

Playtester

Draw opening hands, mulligan and goldfish your deck. On a big screen it becomes a full table: drag cards anywhere, tap, and move between zones.

favorite

Life tracker

1 to 8 players with commander damage, counters, monarch, initiative and dungeons, day and night, turn timers and high rolls.

qr_code_2

Share decks

Send a deck as a link or QR code. Friends with Cosmium open it in the app; everyone else sees the list in their browser.

auto_awesome

Ideas for your decks

Suggestions for your Commander decks from EDHREC, newest sets first, and a rules lookup for any keyword.

Your data stays yours

Everything lives on your device. Cosmium has no server, no accounts of its own, no ads and no tracking.

Want your phone and PC to match? Sign in with Google and Cosmium syncs through a private folder in your own Google Drive that only Cosmium can see, with a daily backup.
