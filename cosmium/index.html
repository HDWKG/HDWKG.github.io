Cosmium

Decks · Collection · Card scanner · Card search · Life counter

Cosmium is a free app for Magic: The Gathering players, for Android and Windows. It keeps your decks and your collection in one place, finds cards fast, and runs your games.

What it does

  • Card search with full filters, every printing, rulings and prices (Card Kingdom, TCGplayer, Cardmarket).
  • Deck builder for Commander and other formats: stats, mana base help, budget swaps, EDHREC ideas, and import from Archidekt, Moxfield and text lists.
  • Card scanner: point the camera at your cards to add them, with the right version, language and finish, to a binder, a list or a deck.
  • Collection tracker with folders and lists, values and gains, and CSV import and export.
  • Life tracker for 1 to 8 players: commander damage, counters, monarch, initiative and dungeons, day and night, turn timers and high rolls.
  • Optional Google sign-in to keep your data the same on your phone and PC, with daily backups in your own Google Drive.

Your data

Everything is kept on your device. Cosmium has no server, no accounts of its own and no ads. If you choose to sign in with Google, your data is synced through a private folder in your own Google Drive that only Cosmium can see. Read the privacy policy for the details.
