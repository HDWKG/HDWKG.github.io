Privacy policy

Cosmium · Last updated 18 November 2026

Cosmium ("the app") is a free Magic: The Gathering deck builder, collection tracker, card scanner, card search, playtester and life counter for Android and Windows, made by an individual developer ("I"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. The short version: your data stays on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you choose to sign in. I don't run a server, the app has no ads or analytics, and I never receive your data unless you email me an error report yourself.

Data stored on your device

Your decks, collection, folders, settings, life tracker games, recently viewed cards and cached card data and prices are stored only on your device. You can export them to a backup file, and you can delete them by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Camera (card scanner)

The card scanner uses your camera only while it is open, after you allow camera access. The pictures are read on your device to find the card's name, set code and number; no picture is saved or sent anywhere. Only the name, set code and number that were read are sent to Scryfall to look the card up, like a card search. The microphone is never used.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can use the app without signing in. If you sign in with Google, the app asks for:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile): only shown in the app's Settings so you can see which account is signed in. They are stored on your device and nowhere else.
  • Access to Cosmium's own app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata): a hidden folder that only Cosmium can read and write. The app uses it to store one synced copy of your Cosmium data and up to 7 daily backups, so your phone and PC stay in step and you can restore your data. The app cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

This data goes directly between your device and Google. It is not sent to me or to anyone else, it is not sold, and it is not used for ads.

Limited Use: Cosmium's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your Cosmium account data

Cosmium has no account of its own: signing in only connects the app to your Google account, and I have no server holding your data. To remove everything Cosmium has stored for your account, follow these steps:

  • Sign out in the app's Settings to stop syncing on that device.
  • Delete the synced data and backups: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → Cosmium → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Cosmium's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the data on your device: uninstall the app or clear its storage.

What is deleted: the synced copy of your decks, collection and settings, all daily backups, and the name, email address and profile picture stored in the app. These are deleted immediately when you take the steps above; nothing is kept.

What is kept: nothing by Cosmium. The only exception is an error report you chose to email me: it stays in my inbox until I delete it. Email cosmium.app@gmail.com and I'll delete your reports within 30 days. Your Google account itself is not affected.

Sharing a deck

When you share a deck as a link or QR code, the decklist itself is packed into the link. It is not uploaded anywhere: whoever you give the link to can read the deck, and nobody else. Opening such a link in a browser shows the list on this website; the deck stays in the part of the link after the #, which browsers don't send to the server.

Error reports

If the app hits an error, it keeps a short log of it on your device. The app may ask whether you want to send it; nothing is sent unless you choose to. If you do, the report opens in your own email app so you can read and edit it first. It contains the app version, your operating system version and the error details (which can mention things like a card name), but not your decks, collection or account. It also reaches me from your email address. I only use it to fix the problem.

Other services the app talks to

To work, the app downloads public data from these services. They receive the normal information of any web request (such as your IP address) and, for searches, what you searched for. No account details are sent.

  • Scryfall: card search, card data, images and sets.
  • MTGJSON: Card Kingdom price files.
  • EDHREC: commander recommendations.
  • Commander Spellbook: combos in a deck (the deck's card names are sent to find them).
  • Wizards of the Coast: the Comprehensive Rules, for the rules lookup.
  • Archidekt and Moxfield: only when you import a deck from a link.
  • Google Fonts: the app's fonts.
  • Card Kingdom, TCGplayer and Cardmarket: only when you open a store link, in your browser.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
