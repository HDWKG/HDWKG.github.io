Privacy policy

Spellvault · Last updated 14 November 2026

Spellvault ("the app") is a free Magic: The Gathering deck builder, collection tracker, card search and life counter for Android and Windows, made by an individual developer ("I"). This policy explains what data the app uses and why. The short version: your data stays on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you choose to sign in. I don't run a server and I never receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Your decks, collection, folders, settings, life tracker games, recently viewed cards and cached card data and prices are stored only on your device. You can export them to a backup file, and you can delete them by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can use the app without signing in. If you sign in with Google, the app asks for:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile): only shown in the app's Settings so you can see which account is signed in. They are stored on your device and nowhere else.
  • Access to Spellvault's own app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata): a hidden folder that only Spellvault can read and write. The app uses it to store one synced copy of your Spellvault data and up to 7 daily backups, so your phone and PC stay in step and you can restore your data. The app cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

This data goes directly between your device and Google. It is not sent to me or to anyone else, it is not sold, and it is not used for ads.

Limited Use: Spellvault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your Google data

  • Sign out in the app's Settings to stop syncing on that device.
  • Delete the synced data and backups: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → Spellvault → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Spellvault's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other services the app talks to

To work, the app downloads public data from these services. They receive the normal information of any web request (such as your IP address) and, for searches, what you searched for. No account details are sent.

  • Scryfall: card search, card data, images and sets.
  • MTGJSON: Card Kingdom price files.
  • EDHREC: commander recommendations.
  • Archidekt and Moxfield: only when you import a deck from a link.
  • Google Fonts: the app's fonts.
  • Card Kingdom, TCGplayer and Cardmarket: only when you open a store link, in your browser.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
